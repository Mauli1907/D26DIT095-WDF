TechFest Spoural 2026

📅 Date: Sunday, November 15, 2026
⏰ Time: 09:00 AM
📍 Venue: FTE Auditorium & Central Lawns

Full Description

TechFest Spoural 2026 is the premier annual national-level technical festival of Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT), organized by the Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE).

Featuring an exhilarating 24-hour hackathon, competitive coding sprints, autonomous robotics combat, drone racing, web UI design showcases, and expert panels led by tech leaders. Top winning teams receive trophies, certificates of merit, and cash prizes worth over ₹1,50,000.

Participation Guidelines

  • Open to all registered undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students.
  • Official CHARUSAT student identity cards must be presented during registration verification.
  • Certificates of participation will be awarded to all attendees.

Event Details

  • Total Capacity: 250 Students
  • Confirmed Seats: 48
  • Seats Remaining: 202
  • Registration Fee: Free
  • Eligibility: All FTE Departments
