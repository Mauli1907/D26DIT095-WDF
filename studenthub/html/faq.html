Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about StudentHub portal features, registrations, account security, and lab practicals.

StudentHub is the unified digital portal for students of Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT), FTE. It streamlines event registrations, notices, profiles, and campus updates.

Click on the 'Register' link in the top menu. Enter your name, official college email, mobile number, department/course, solve the Canvas CAPTCHA, and submit.

Browse upcoming workshops or hackathons on the Events page, click on any event card, and click 'Register for Event'. Your seat is confirmed instantly.

All passwords are encrypted using PHP's password_hash() with BCrypt (cost factor 10) before database storage. Plaintext passwords are never saved.

Click the sun/moon button in the top navigation bar. Your theme choice is stored in your browser's localStorage and retained across visits.
